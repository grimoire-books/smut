# Chapter 22
## Lean

Joss finds him after a day that was only standing behind.

"PE," Joss says, from the doorway.

The unnamed man looks at him. The study is a door down the hall. The word is in the house. The hall was a Thursday that already happened.

"If I say no," he says.

"I hear it. I'm asking this time. That's the fashion. Come upstairs or tell me to fuck off and I'll go and be civil at the toast."

He could fuck off. He does not. He says, "Aye. Asking. Kettles still stops you."

"It still stops me." Joss's mouth is a line that wants to be a smile. "I hated it. I will hate it again if you use it. I'll still stop. That's the box I am in. Come here."

Upstairs. Door shut. Joss's room: expensive soap, the animal, the bed that has learned them. No belt on the chair tonight. Joss takes his mouth standing, hand in the hair, and he leans into it because leaning is the graph now.

Then the bed. Face-down, then not — Joss turns him, looks at him, which is still the part Joss was trying not to buy. Forearm across the chest, not the throat. Joss fucks him and talks. Breathe. Take it. That's the work. The body learns what the mouth won't. The words are PE and they are also Joss not knowing what else to do with a man who asked.

He could BIND him. The latch is habit now. He does not. Hulme was ten. This is not Hulme.

Joss reaches for the wrist after, the Priya-place, the rumour, and he flinches hard enough that Joss's hand stops in the air.

"Don't."

Joss hangs there. The boy in the picture, briefly.

"Kettles?" Joss says, and it costs him.

"No. Just — not the wrist. Not tonight."

Joss takes the hand away. Puts it in his hair instead. Starts to move again. The flinch sits in the room with them and does not become a funeral. That is the new information. The body remembers the study. The mouth still has a word. The word was not required. A no about a wrist was enough.

Joss comes inside him. Stays. Breathing ugly. Does not bite the shoulder that has been wrong since the dream. That, too, is new, or it is Joss trying.

"You leaned," Joss says, into his throat.

"Aye."

"That's the making. Not the hall. Not his rope. You came upstairs and you leaned and you didn't wreck me. I am going to be insufferable about it."

"I know."

"Get up."

He gets up. Shower. Rust water. The wrist was not marked. The flinch was. He sleeps in the back room with the door shut because the graph is still a graph and an allowed bed is still a bed and he needs them not to be one room.

Morning Joss civil in a shirt. Tea on the table.

"Eat. You look like work."

He eats. Priya looks at both of them and does not ask. Cairn, from the study door, looks at Joss and then at him and says nothing, which is a kind of useful. The lean is the whole day. The word is still in the house. He did not have to spend it. That is not the Study. That is the point.
