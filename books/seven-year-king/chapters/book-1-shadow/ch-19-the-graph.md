# Chapter 19
## The Graph

The study sits in the house after.

Not a speech. A flinch when Cairn's door is ajar. A lean toward it anyway, which is worse. He does the candle in the back room without being told. Smaller. Stay in the room. The rope is on the desk behind a door he does not open.

Cairn finds him at the table with the radio on.

"Curriculum continues. You will stand behind me. You will sit with the candle. You will not make last night into a religion or a reason to leave. Eat."

He eats. The tea tastes of the mug. Joss watches him wash a plate and does not put him on his knees. That is not kindness. That is Cairn's look from the study door.

Priya puts a list on the fridge. Milk. Bread. Tomatoes that have never seen a vine. He takes the list to Tesco because standing still is worse. The flinch comes in the cereal aisle for no reason and goes. He pays. He walks back under the hedge.

At night there is no crow on the wall. He does not tell the house about the train. He does not tell them about a look that had a date in it. Curriculum. Cairn's word. It is not a school. It is a graph he is being drawn on.

He sleeps. He gets up. Warehouse. Mark. Kettles into boxes. At four he looks at the other bus and does not take it. Palatine. The door opens before he knocks. He wipes his feet.

Once is not a habit. He holds that in his mouth with the tea. He leans anyway.
