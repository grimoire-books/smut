# Chapter 12
## Habit

Cairn starts putting him on people the small way.

Not Ancoats. A wrist in the kitchen. A man in a car park who owes the house and pays in the mouth. BIND as a habit, like taping a box.

"Feel it," Cairn says. "Don't take the room. Hold the edge."

He holds the edge. The leak wants the whole tap. He keeps it to the pulse. The man makes a sound and goes slack and Cairn says enough and the man walks away on his own feet.

After, the stone in his pocket — a bit of canal wall he picked up without deciding to — sits warm in his palm like it has been near a fire. He puts it on the windowsill in the back room. It stays warm longer than stone should.

Priya finds him in the kitchen at midnight with his hands under the tap.

"You're humming," she says.

"I'm not."

"You are." She takes his wrist the way she does, a look first, then the cut, then her mouth. The BIND that is habit now sits between them without anyone naming it. She fucks him against the counter because that is the other half of the same verb and she does not make it a lesson. Short. He comes with his mouth on her shoulder. She follows, quiet, and puts the kettle on after as if that were the point.

"That's enough for a night," she says.

Joss, passing: "He's getting house-trained."

Priya does not look at him. "Go to bed."

In the back room the stone is still warm. He leaves it there. He does not tell Cairn. He does not tell the woman on the train.

The habit is in his hands when he packs kettles in the morning. Mark tells him not to drop them. He does not.
