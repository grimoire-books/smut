# Chapter 17
## The Back Room

Aftermath sits.

That is all this room is for, tonight. Sheet. Hedge. The bag. He has showered and the shower has not taken the carpet off his face. Tea on the floor because Priya put it there and left, and did not ask about the word, and did not look at his wrists.

He drinks it when it is cold.

The study is two rooms away and still open in him. Rope on a desk. A latch that did not lift when he used the house word. Kettles said. Heard. Kept. That is the fact he brought down the stairs. He does not put it on the sheet.

Joss puts her head in and sees him and does not come in.

"He used the study," Joss says.

"Aye."

Joss's mouth does a thing that is not a smile. "I told you PE was the kind version. I stop. He doesn't. That was the whole of the lesson, if you were listening."

"Go away, Joss."

Joss goes. That is new. Or it is Cairn's door still shut and Joss knowing which floorboards creak.

He sits on the bed. The wrists are not marked. The room is. He can feel it in the floor the way he felt the stone when he did not put his hand on it. He could pack the bag. The bag is already packed. Packing it again would be a performance.

Drew comes later and sits on the end of the bed without asking and does not take a wrist.

"Still here," Drew says.

"Aye."

"That's the work. He opens you. You don't leave. I didn't leave. Priya didn't. Joss didn't, which surprises people." Drew picks at the sheet. "I'm not going to say mine tonight. You'd bite me."

He almost smiles. He does not.

"Did you use it."

"Aye."

"And."

"He heard it."

Drew nods as if that were a weather report he already had. Stays until the tea is gone. Puts the mug on the sill next to the bit of canal wall and does not ask what the wall is for.

"Sleep. I'll be on the sofa. Not because you asked."

He goes, and the house ticks, and he lies down on top of the sheet because getting under it feels like agreeing to something he has not named.

He sleeps. He does not dream the ford. He dreams the rope on the desk, which is worse, and wakes with an erection and a hate that is not a vote, and the hedge is a hedge, and his hands are only hands.

In the morning the wrists are not marked. The room is. He gets up anyway.

Kitchen. Radio. Priya does not look at his face for long. She puts toast down. Butter. The kettle, which is a kettle.

"Eat."

He eats. Cairn is not at the table. The study door is shut. Joss comes down in a shirt and is civil, which is its own weather.

"You lasted," Joss says, to the toast. "Don't make it a personality."

He washes the plate. He goes to the warehouse. Mark does not ask. He packs. He comes back. He wipes his feet. The back room is still the back room. The bag is still a bag. He does not unpack it. He does not leave.
