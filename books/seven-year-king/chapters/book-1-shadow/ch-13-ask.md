# Chapter 13
## Ask

Joss calls it PE.

The cellar under the house. Mat that smells of dust. A bar that has been used. Joss in a shirt that will not survive the hour.

"On your back. Knees up. Don't be precious."

It is not a gym. It is Joss's hands and Joss's weight and Joss's mouth saying hold and again and don't you dare go soft.

He says no once, when Joss's hand goes where the hall went.

Joss hears it.

Joss does not stop.

"That's the point," Joss says into his ear. "You can say it. I can hear you. We keep going. Cairn wants standing. Standing is this."

He could wreck Joss on the mat. The line is there, the way it was in the hall. He does not take it. Joss fucks him on the mat until he comes shaking and Joss comes after, biting his shoulder through the jumper Priya put on the chair.

After, Joss sits on his heels and looks at him as if marking a sheet.

"You said no."

"Aye."

"And here you still are." Joss wipes his mouth. "That's the training. Don't look at me like I invented it. I am the part Cairn does not want to do himself."

He showers in the rust-smelling water that is not rust here, that is only old pipes. The bite will show at the warehouse. He puts the jumper back on.

Upstairs Priya looks at the bite and puts a plate down harder than pasta deserves.

"PE," Joss says, passing, cheerful.

Priya looks at Cairn's study door. Does not knock.

He eats. The no is still in his mouth. It did not do a job.

He goes to work.
