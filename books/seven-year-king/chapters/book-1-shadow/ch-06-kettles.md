# Chapter 6
## The Candle

The back room is a table, a chair, a candle in a saucer that has already known wax.

Cairn sits opposite him as if this were accounts.

"Again."

He looks at the flame. The leak sits up. The flame leans toward him.

"Smaller. You go out like a drunk. Stay in the room. Don't smash Green in my kitchen. I have a house to keep."

He pulls back. The candle gutters and then decides to live. His nose drips. Cairn puts a handkerchief on the table and does not watch him use it.

"There is one room. This one. The rest is other people's. You will not like what happens when you take theirs without a door."

"The girl."

"Fallowfield. You entered her. Not only the ordinary way. That is a door. You do not puppet a body like a box. You meet a mind. You were lucky she was only bleeding. You were lucky I found you before a mouth that does not make tea."

"I said no. In the hall."

"I heard. Joss heard. Hearing is not a door." Cairn looks at him over the glass. "Priya gave you a word. That is her religion. It will work on Joss if you use it. It will work on Drew. It will work on her. Do not bring it to me as if I were a flatmate. I am not going to pretend this coven is a negotiation. The hall was me. The Study, when it comes, will be me. "I heard you the first time."

He sits on the bed in the back room until his hands are only hands. The candle is out. The word is in his mouth like a taste. It worked. That is not the same as safe.

The house holds.

That is not a door out.
