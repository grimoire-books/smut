# Chapter 18
## The Table

Morning. He goes downstairs because staying up is a kind of dying he has not agreed to, and because Cairn said a plate on the knee, and because the leash is the getting up.

The kitchen is a kitchen. Toast. Butter. Priya's radio on too low. Drew not here, or here and told to be elsewhere. Joss at the counter, not eating, looking at the unnamed man as if looking could undo rope. Cairn in the study doorway. The unnamed man does not look at him first. He looks at the table. He sits. Priya puts a plate down. He eats. The eating is a job. The jam is too sweet. He eats it anyway.

Cairn says, "Candle at nine."

Priya says, "No."

Cairn says, "The door will try to open. He needs the muscle."

The unnamed man says, "I'll do the candle." His voice is a stranger. "Don't talk about me as a tap."

Cairn nods, once. He does not say sorry again. The sorry in the study is still sitting there; bringing it down to jam would make it a joke. There is no joke. He goes back to the map. The unnamed man feels the flinch in his own neck when the study door moves, and hates the flinch, and eats.

Joss says, "You don't have to be useful today."

"I do. That's the—" He still cannot say leash. "That's living here."

Joss's mouth is a line. "I will not be civil to him."

"That's your marriage," the unnamed man says, and it is almost the old sport, and it dies, and Joss looks like the boy in the picture, and Priya turns the radio up one notch, which is mercy.

He does the candle at nine. Cairn in the room, not touching. The unnamed man feels the candle. Only the candle. The stone is quiet. He does not look at Cairn's hands. When it is over Cairn says, "Useful," and the word is the dock, and the unnamed man walks out before it can become anything else.

---

Days, not a montage. A flinch when Drew says *stay* in the kitchen, laughing, and Drew's face falling, and Priya saying, later, "He knows enough, not all of it." A flinch when Joss's fingers find his hair on the sofa, and then he leans into it, because Joss stopped, and the leaning is a vote he is allowed. Asked taking: none, for a week. Priya does not ask. Drew does not ask. Cairn does not ask. The not-asking is its own temperature. He is still ours. He still eats. He still stands behind Cairn once, on a street in Salford, a closed tap, no rope, and after he has to sit on a wall and wait for his hands, and Cairn waits with him and does not speak, and that is not aftercare and is not not.

Ruth, at the corner, tote, sees his face and does not talk too much, which is how he knows he looks like a warning. She says, "I'm sorry," without knowing, and he says, "Don't be," and she says, "The stone's quiet. That's not always a kindness," and he walks home, and home is the table, and the table is a collar, and both are true.

He sleeps in the back room. Once he sleeps in Joss's bed because Joss asks, please, no belt, and he says aye, and they do not fuck, and Joss's arm is careful, and in the night the unnamed man flinches at a hand on his wrist that is only Joss asleep, and Joss wakes and takes the hand away without being told, and that, too, is the house.

The crow is on the hedge. He does not nod. He does not tell it. He thinks, once, of a woman on a platform who looked at him like a time, and he does not go to Victoria to find a train. He puts a plate on his knee. He is useful. He hates Cairn in the mornings and less at four o'clock and more at night, which is not forgiveness, it is a graph. He does not tidy the graph.

Cairn, at the study door, a week on: "I will not do that again. Once is the point. Twice is a habit. I am not building a habit of that. I am building a door staying shut. If the door opens I will find another way. I will not tell you it was for your own good. It was for the house. You may leave. I would rather you didn't. That is the whole of my kindness. It is not a large kindness. It never was."

The unnamed man says, "I'm not leaving."

"I know." Cairn's tired face. "That's the leash. I am not proud of it. I am using it. Both of those can sit too."

They sit. The radio. The map. The stone, quiet. The seed, not quiet, but not a river tonight: a chair, a table, a crow, a man who said no and came anyway and got up in the morning, which is not a moral, which is a life, which is Book 1 still being team hunger, still being ours, still unnamed, still a leak, still a weapon sheathed, the sheath a little more worn, the yet a little closer to a when, and not yet, not yet, not yet.
