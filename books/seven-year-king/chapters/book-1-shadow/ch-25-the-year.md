# Chapter 25
## The Year

Rain. Then a week of heat that makes the Palatine brick smell. Then rain again.

He is still packing. He is still at the table. The back room is where he sleeps unless Drew says stay or Priya's door is open or Joss has not left the hall. The damp flat gets a standing order and then a text he does not answer and then a silence that is a kind of eviction he did not have to arrange.

Mark says he looks like someone who has a kitchen. He packs. The leak sits. The candle lessons get smaller. Cairn says stay in the room and means this one.

A Tuesday the heating dies. Priya swears at the boiler like it is Joss. Joss comes down in a towel and fixes it because Joss can fix a boiler and will not admit he learned it anywhere kind. Drew puts a record on at last, something old, and the house has a noise that is not the radio. Cairn does not come out of the study. The crow is on the hedge. He does not tell it about the boiler.

A Thursday he almost says a name that is not a name he has given. Priya's mouth is on his throat in the kitchen and she says hey, which is what she says, and he almost answers with something that would sit on a form. He does not. The almost sits in his teeth. She does not ask.

One night the house is all of them.

Not the study. Not the blood. Radio. Whiskey that is not good. The sitting room for once, not a bedroom claimed. Priya in his lap on the sofa that has accepted him. Drew's mouth on his throat. Joss watching from the armchair and then not watching, then in, teeth, laugh. Cairn in the doorway with a glass, not joining, not leaving.

"Kettles is in the room," Priya says, to the room, not to a lesson. "If anyone needs it they say it. I'm not running a class."

Nobody says it.

She kisses him first, slow, her hand in his hair like the first night without the inventory. He is hard against her and she feels it and does not make a speech. She gets her skirt out of the way and takes him into her on the sofa with Drew still at his throat and Joss's hand arriving on his chest as if a chest were a thing you could share.

It is not the Study. It is not the hall. It is weather. Belonging-hot. Someone laughs into his shoulder. Drew says mine and Priya says that's not your sentence tonight and Drew laughs, sincere, and does not stop his mouth.

Joss waits until Priya has had what she came for and climbed off, wrecked-fond, and then Joss takes him down onto the rug because Joss does not like sofas. Hands. A bite that will be a problem under a collar. He could say the word. He does not. Joss fucks him like a grudge that has decided to live here, and Drew watches and then does not watch, and Priya puts a glass of water on the floor where a person can reach it.

Cairn drinks in the doorway. He does not come in. He does not look away.

He comes more than once. He loses the count. Someone says a name that is not a name he has given. He does not take it.

After, the kitchen floor. Toast at an hour that is not morning. Priya's pencil back in her hair. Joss asleep on the sofa like a knife put down. Drew saying stay to the room.

Cairn: "You lasted a year in my house. That is not nothing. The rest is next. Not tonight."

He sleeps where he drops.

In the morning he goes to work. Mark tells him he looks like shite. He packs. The year is a year. The blood has not happened. Nobody winks. The jumper on the chair is a jumper. The shelf is still empty. He wipes his feet because the mat is there.

Leaving would hurt. That is no longer a sentence he has to say. It is the boiler, the record, the toast, the almost-name he did not give. It is a year.
