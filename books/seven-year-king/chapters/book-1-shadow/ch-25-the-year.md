# Chapter 25
## The Year

The year does a year.

Rain. Then a week of heat that makes the Palatine brick smell. Then rain again. He is still packing kettles. He is still at the table. The back room is where he sleeps unless Drew says stay or Priya's door is open or Joss has not left the hall.

One night the house is all of them.

Not the study. Not the blood. Radio. Whiskey that is not good. Priya in his lap. Drew's mouth on his throat. Joss watching and then not watching, then in, teeth, laugh. Cairn in the doorway with a glass, not joining, not leaving.

He comes more than once. He loses the count. Someone says a name that is not a name he has given. He does not take it.

After, the kitchen floor. Toast at an hour that is not morning. Priya's pencil back in her hair. Joss asleep on the sofa like a knife put down. Drew saying stay to the room.

Cairn: "You lasted a year in my house. That is not nothing. The rest is next. Not tonight."

He sleeps where he drops.

In the morning he goes to work. Mark tells him he looks like shite. He packs. The year is a year. The blood has not happened. Nobody winks.
