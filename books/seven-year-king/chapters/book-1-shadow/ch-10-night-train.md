# Chapter 10
## Night Train

Night bus first, then the last train because the bus gave up.

He stands because the seats are wet. The carriage is three people and a smell of chip fat. His shoulder sits wrong. The leak is quieter than Ancoats and still there.

A woman at the far end looks at him.

Not the way Sian looked. Not the way Joss looks. She looks at him as if he is a time she already spent. Coat. Dark hair. No smile. The window behind her is black and her face is in it twice.

The train takes the river and the brick and a stretch of nothing.

She does not come down the carriage. She does not speak. When the doors open at his stop he almost misses it because he is waiting for her to do something.

She does not.

He gets off.

On the platform he looks back. She is still in the glass. Then the train takes her.

He does not follow.

He does not have a word for why he wanted to.

Palatine. Green door. The house is a radio on low and Drew laughing at something that is not him. He wipes his feet. Priya looks up from the sink.

"You're late."

"Train."

"There's plate."

He eats standing. The woman is a face in glass. He does not put her in a sentence for the table.

In the back room he lies with the bag still packed at the corners and waits to dream the ford.

He gets the river. Not the ford. Dark glass. A look that already knows the morning.

He does not follow that either.
