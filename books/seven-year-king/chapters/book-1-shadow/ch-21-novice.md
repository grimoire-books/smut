# Chapter 21
## Novice

Cairn makes the week visible.

Candle. Stand-behind. Joss. Table. Not the pub. Not Ruth's green. Not the study twice. Nights he is furniture. Nights he is the hand. He is still meat. He is less surprised by it.

Joss is the instrument. Cairn designs. That sentence is not said. It is the week.

Hulme. Another house. Brick that has given up being proud. A man in a kitchen who will not stop talking — names, debts, a woman in Chorlton, a night he saw something in a canal and did not walk away. Cairn listens with the face he uses for maps. Then he looks at the unnamed man.

He comes forward. Latch. BIND. A count of ten. The talking stops as if a tap had been closed. The man blinks. Cairn finishes what Cairn came to finish: a paper signed, a door that will stay shut, a smell in the hall that is not their smell. He lets go. The man does not know what he lost. He knows he is tired.

In the car Joss says, "Pretty."

Cairn says, "Useful. Do not confuse them."

Joss looks at the unnamed man in the mirror. "He didn't smash it."

"He stayed in the room. That is the work." Cairn does not turn. "You will not take him in this car. You will not call him pretty when he has just been a tool. He is still meat. Meat that can count to ten is not a graduate."

Outsider to peer is not a ceremony. A man in Ancoats nodding at him instead of at Cairn. Priya handing him the list without the speech. Drew saying ours at the door and meaning the four of them and him. A woman at the corner shop who used to look through him and now looks away, which is Manchester manners learning a new fact.

He does not go to the warehouse. That door thinned. Mark does not text. The packing-line woman is someone else's problem. Soon work would have been a crime scene. It is not, because he left before the leak learned the size of a shift.

At the table Cairn says, "You are not a guest. Guests leave plates. You will wash. You will stand where I put you. You will not mistake Joss for the design. He is a blade. I hold the handle."

Joss shows his teeth at the toast. Priya does not laugh. The unnamed man washes the plates. The week has a shape. That is the novice, if it needs a word. It does not.

Later, in the back room, he sits with the canal stone. Warm. Not as warm as the field. He does not tell Cairn. He does the candle smaller. He does not reach Hulme. He does not reach the field. He reaches the kitchen, the radio, Joss's door ajar, Priya's pencil on the sill. Stay in the room. The room is this house. He is in it. Still meat. Less surprised.
