# Chapter 21
## Novice

Cairn makes the week visible.

Candle. Stand-behind. Joss. Table. Not the pub. Not Ruth's green. Not the study twice. Nights he is furniture. Nights he is the hand. He is still meat. He is less surprised by it.

Joss is the instrument. Cairn designs. That sentence is not said. It is the week.

Hulme. Another house. A man who will not stop talking. Cairn looks at him. He comes forward. Latch — BIND, a count of ten — and the talking stops. Cairn finishes what Cairn came to finish. He lets go. The man blinks and does not know what he lost.

In the car Joss says, "Pretty."

Cairn says, "Useful. Do not confuse them."

Outsider to peer is not a ceremony. A man in Ancoats nodding at him instead of at Cairn. Priya handing him the list without the speech. Drew saying *ours* at the door and meaning the four of them and him.

He still goes to the warehouse. Mark still calls four early. The packing-line woman still will not stand next to him. Soon work is a crime scene. Not yet. Smaller.

At the table Cairn says, "You are not a guest. Guests leave plates. You will wash. You will stand where I put you. You will not mistake Joss for the design. He is a blade. I hold the handle."

Joss shows his teeth at the toast. Priya does not laugh. He washes the plates. The week has a shape. That is the novice, if it needs a word. It does not.
