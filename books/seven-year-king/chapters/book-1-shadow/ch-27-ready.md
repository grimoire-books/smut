# Chapter 27
## Ready

Cairn says it in the kitchen, same as tea.

"The blood is next."

Joss looks pleased in a way that is not kind. Priya's hand stops on the butter dish and then starts again. Drew is on the stairs and does not come down. The radio is doing the weather. Rain later. Rain now.

"Not tonight," Cairn says. "Soon. You will take ours. We will take yours. After, you last. That is the making I have been pointing you at since the dock."

He eats. The tea tastes of the mug. Toast gone cold at the edge. He puts butter on it anyway.

"Will it hurt."

Joss laughs. "Yes."

Priya: "You can still walk."

Cairn: "He will not walk. He has not walked since Palatine. Do not be sentimental at my table."

Nobody says it will fail. He does not know to look for that sentence.

He goes to the warehouse. Mark looks at his face and does not ask. He packs. Kettles go into boxes. Boxes go onto pallets. The leak sits in him and does not smash. A woman two rollers down will not stand next to him. She has not stood next to him for months. He tapes. He comes home with cardboard dust on his cuffs and wipes his feet because the mat is there.

The study door is shut.

Half-made, Joss says later, in the hall, not touching him. Almost a person. Almost ours. Don't embarrass me when it happens.

"I don't know what it is."

"You don't have to. You have to last. That's the job. The rest is Cairn's."

Joss's mouth does a thing that might have been a smile if it had been anyone else. He goes upstairs. Music through the floor, two rooms, then nothing.

Priya puts a plate in front of him that he did not ask for. Leftovers. She sits. She does not take his wrist.

"You can still walk," she says again, quieter. "I'm not Cairn. I'm saying it because I want it to stay true."

"Aye."

She nods as if that were a plan. She does not say stay. That is Drew's word. She does not say kettles. The word is for other rooms. She gets up and washes the dish she just used.

Drew comes down late and looks at all of them and does not say mine. That is how he knows it is close.

He sleeps in the back room. The bag is still a bag. The jumper from the dresser is on the chair. He does not put it on. The shelf they will fill later is empty. He does not dream the ford. He dreams a kitchen that will not keep butter, and wakes, and the hedge is a hedge.

Ready is not a feeling he can find a word for. It is the plate. It is the mat. It is not walking. He gets up. He goes to work. He comes back. The door opens. He wipes his feet.
