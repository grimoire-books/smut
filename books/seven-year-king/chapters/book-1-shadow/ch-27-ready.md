# Chapter 27
## Ready

Cairn says it in the kitchen, same as tea.

"The blood is next."

Joss looks pleased in a way that is not kind. Priya's hand stops on the butter dish and then starts again. Drew is on the stairs and does not come down. The radio is on.

"Not tonight," Cairn says. "Soon. You will take ours. We will take yours. After, you last. That is the making I have been pointing you at since the dock."

He eats. The tea tastes of the mug.

"Will it hurt."

Joss laughs. "Yes."

Priya: "You can still walk."

Cairn: "He will not walk. He has not walked since Palatine. Do not be sentimental at my table."

Nobody says it will fail. He does not know to look for that sentence.

He goes to the warehouse. He packs. He comes home. The study door is shut.

Half-made, Joss says later, in the hall, not touching him. Almost a person. Almost ours. Don't embarrass me when it happens.

He sleeps in the back room.

The jumper from the dresser is on the chair.

He does not put it on.
 down late and looks at all of them and does not say mine. That is how he knows it is close.

He sleeps in the back room. The bag is still a bag. The shelf they will fill later is empty. He does not dream the ford. He dreams a kitchen that will not keep butter, and wakes, and the hedge is a hedge.
