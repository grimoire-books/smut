# Chapter 27
## Ready

Cairn finds him at the study door, not in the ropes. That is a different country.

"A word," Cairn says, which is how the dock started, and the unnamed man goes in because the house has taught him that a word is how useful begins. The door that is never quite shut is not shut. No rope on the chair. The map. The dark glass.

"You're ready for the blood," Cairn says. Not a question. "Not tonight. Soon. Sex and exchange. The old way. I will sire it. Joss will be in the room because Joss is the ugly part and this is not a kindness. Priya will not be in the room because Priya would try to make it a kindness. Drew already knows how it feels. You do not. You will hate parts of it. That is the point. After, you will be ours properly. Childer. The word is Drew's. The fact is mine."

The unnamed man hears *childer* and the leak sits up and his mouth does not say no. He has been standing in cellars for a year. He has said kettles. He has said aye. He has not left. The house required this. He believes that.

"What if I say no."

"Then you walk out of my study and I wish you luck I do not have to give. The luck will not hold. You opened. The city has a nose. I am not the only nose. I would rather you were ours than a mess I have to mop."

"Aye."

Cairn looks at him a long time. "Do not look at me as if I have told you a secret about failing. I have told you the next thing. You will stand. You have learned standing. The blood is standing of a different kind."

He does not know it will fail. Nobody has told him. There is no wink. The crow is not in the study.

Joss, in the kitchen, is pleased in a way that is almost a person. "About time. I was getting bored of the cellar."

Priya's face does a grief so small it would not photograph. She puts a plate down harder than the plate deserves. She does not argue in front of Joss. Later, in the back room, she says, "You can say no."

"I know."

"Are you going to."

He thinks of the leak. The bus. Sian's blood. The crow. The heating. The cellar. The chair. The table. "No. I'm going to say aye."

"Then I will be here after. I will not be in the room. I will be here after. That's the thing I can build."

Drew, passing the door: "It's not so bad. You come out the other side. I did. I'm fun." He says it like a joke. Priya does not laugh. The unnamed man files Drew too, deeper: already forged, graduate-speak, KEEP waiting on the far side of a fact that has not happened yet.

He sleeps. He does not dream the ford. He dreams the table. He wakes. The blood is not tonight. Soon. He is half-made. He believes they will own him properly. He is still ours. The crow is on the hedge. He does not tell it. He puts a plate on his knee. He is useful. He is ready, which is a word Cairn used, which is not the same as safe, which is not the same as the Deal, which is a country he has not heard of, which is just as well.
