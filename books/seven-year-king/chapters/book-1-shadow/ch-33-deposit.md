# Chapter 33
## Deposit

They put things on the shelf in the back room as if a shelf were a future.

A key that is not the Palatine key. A photograph Drew prints off a phone: a kitchen that is not this kitchen, winter light, a window onto a field that might be Wales if you squint and do not ask Cairn. A jumper Priya says will fit him when he is sick of this one. Joss puts nothing on the shelf and then puts a lighter there and does not explain.

"Later," Drew says. Cheerful, which is worse. "Not now. After Joss has stopped being a knife every Tuesday. We keep him. Look. Evidence."

Cairn does not bless the shelf. Cairn looks at it from the doorway and says, "Do not make me a calendar. He works. He sleeps here. The rest is vanity."

Priya touches the jumper once and leaves it.

"It's a week," she says, to the shelf more than to Cairn. "A cooker that works. A radio that is mine. I'm not asking you. I'm building it."

Joss, from the counter: "I'll come. I'll be civil. That's a lie. I'll come anyway."

Drew, from the stairs: "Stay. That's not a winter. That's a leaving. Don't go to a kitchen that isn't this one."

"It's a week," Priya says. "It's a deposit. It's a fact."

He looks at the shelf. He does not take the things down. He does not add the mug with the chip. He does not give them a date. He does not have one. The house thinks it keeps him. The objects think it too.

"Aye," he says.

Priya's face does a thing that is not grief. She puts a folded paper in the drawer with the wooden spoon and the small knife — a listing, a password on the back of a receipt like a person.

"Good. Don't lose that. I wrote it where I write the milk."

He files the drawer, the receipt, the cooker, Joss invited as a joke that stuck, Drew saying stay meaning don't. He plans a life inside a term he has not named yet. Nobody winks. The crow is on the hedge. He does not tell it about the cottage, because the cottage is theirs, and the crow is weather, and later is a fact on a form, and he is still here.

He eats. He goes to the warehouse. He comes back. The shelf is still a shelf. He leaves the lighter where Joss put it.
