# Chapter 31
## The Name on the Air

The name arrives sideways.

Not in the kitchen. Not in Cairn's mouth. On a wet pavement after a job that was not a mess, only a walk. A woman passing who is not the train and not Ruth says it like weather — Morrígan — as if she were naming a pub, and is gone before he can ask who she is.

He does not follow.

He tells nobody. The seed already had three sentences he could not get back. This is a fourth he will not give a news page.

Palatine. Table. Priya looks at his face and does not ask. Joss looks at his face and does.

"Nothing," he says.

Joss does not believe him and lets it go because Cairn is in the doorway.

Cairn: "The land is turning its head. You will not go and meet it without me. Eat."

He eats. The name sits in him like the look on the train. He does not follow that either. In the back room he waits to see if the crow will be on the hedge. It is not. The hedge is a hedge. He sleeps. He does not dream her. He dreams doors shutting.

In the morning the radio does a piece about crows in the city, too many, a man on the phone calling them a nuisance. Priya turns it off. He does not tell her why that was almost funny.
