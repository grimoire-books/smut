5. Magic

LOCK. Clay is the medium. Aether / the between is clay: physics and imagination.

Clay is not a sixth verb. The verbs stay five. Clay is what the verbs work.

Full digest elsewhere is stale where it disagrees. This page is the living lock.

Physics

Energy is real. Conservation. No free lunch.

You pay with life, borrowed life, landscape-life, or unused years.

  • DRAW — pulling clay-stuff through the self.
  • SHAPE — hands in clay. Lift a bus, pay for a lifted bus.
  • BIND — forcing two bodies onto one current.
  • ENTER — going into someone else’s clay / the fork / the other lung.
  • WARP — the body failing to stay a neat pipe.

Belief / imagination

What you can hold and mean, the clay will take as form. Image, name, want, story.

  • Tristan steal: Art / Castles / Chi are belief pressed into clay.
  • Endangered steal: the between is a real layer; overflow; sex cracks concealment.

Belief does not cancel joules. A beautiful image of a lifted bus still costs a lifted bus.

Danu dresses belief as harvest. Shadow dresses it as belonging. She never pretends.

The body is the pipe

All current must run through a body. Nerve, bone, breath, sex, warp.

Magical enhancement widens the pipe. It does not remove it.

Too much current through meat = death. Always. That is physics, not a plot twist.

Morrígan’s chosen always die. How much power she bestows, and the manner of death, vary.

  • Most champions get a night and an iron morning.
  • He gets the largest dose because the job is the largest: unseat both dictators and not sit down.

He never gets a stable god-body. The god-state is the death-state. Do not write: he becomes immortal and then decides to die.

Five verbs

VerbHands in clay
DRAWPull clay-stuff through the self. Earth-side natives have a cap. Otherworld-touched: no cap except what the current lets through. Book 1: he is a leak, not a tank.
SHAPEImpose will on matter and energy. Conservation. High-end: pressure, heat, material, pocket-space.
BINDShare, steal, or gift life-force. Vampire feeding. House taking. Willing gift ≠ theft. Repeatable siphon is how a person becomes nuclear.
ENTERInto someone else’s clay, the fork, the other lung. Mind-work lives here, not in SHAPE. You meet a mind; you do not puppet a body like a rock. Cost: you risk your own Castle. Fear makes alignment harder; intimacy makes it easier.
WARPMeat fails as a neat pipe. Early: partial, ugly. Mid (Deal): Horned God host — Danu’s tap, costume of a god. Final (Book 3): true-god current through the same pipe, then rupture.

Laws that sit on the verbs

  • Direction: most beings work one current. Vampires BIND along Shadow. Witches DRAW/SHAPE along Earth. Shifters are split. He can DRAW both ways (why both flags want him). In Book 3 he draws under both — her kind of current — and that is the death.
  • Sex is BIND + ENTER. Orgasm drops the gate between instinct and will. Her champion-night is the oldest ritual form of this.
  • The seven-year deal is a BIND on the self (True Name as sacrifice). It is Danu’s tap. It is not yet true-god.

How it smells

Earth-current: wet soil, copper, sex, crushed green. Shadow-current: cold iron, old rooms, a mouth after a nosebleed. Clay itself — the between — has no smell. It is the pressure change before weather. People near him when he DRAWs both ways get a nosebleed or an erection or both. That is a feature.

Gifts

A human trait can amplify into a unique mental gift when a person is opened to clay (turned, sworn, or warped). Three gifts in the whole book is plenty. Not everyone gets a party trick.

The Art

Parked under ENTER. Belief pressed into clay. Early: done to him. Mid: he learns it and hates how easy sex makes it. Late: he can do it without sex and chooses not to, except once. Castles nest. A stronger practitioner can suppress someone else’s externalised self — castration-of-self, not a stun. The Shadow King works this at cultural scale: plant one idea until it is policy. Courts and institutions are his Castles.

Gun rule (ENTER + WARP)

Intention to fire locks the line. He feels possibilities, not a HUD, and is not standing there.

Past very short range, aimed fire is almost pointless against a champion with the sense.

Still kills: point-blank; room full of hands; blast / bus / collapse; no-intention ricochet; BIND on the nerves (the study still holds).

Mundanes die to bullets. Leaving the line does not delete the line.

Vampires and witches do not get the fork. Only her sliver.

Book 1: do not retrofit 01–18 example scenes with bullet-time or deity-draw. Gun-rule leak only after the failed turn (wrong door / who-goes-down). Not host. Not true-god. Vampires do not get the fork.

Growth (cards must show this)

  1. Outset — regular person. Meat. No god. No host.
  2. Book 1 leak. Sex cracks concealment (Sian). ENTER flashes. Future-sense is a wrong-door feeling. Not a god.
  3. Book 1 forge. House Vale trains before the blood. Will-strength dictates vampire-strength. Hardship on purpose. Extreme. Still meat. Still a leak.
  4. Late Book 1 — failed turning. Sex ritual + blood exchange. Blood should seize the body. Morrígan sliver burns it out. Unturnable. Battle-body wakes: he matches or beats a strong vampire in the ways that count (speed, strength, place, melee, gun-rule leak only). Still meat. Not host. Not true-god. They wanted a childer. They got a weapon the blood cannot own. Collar stays social: table, bed, BIND, ours.
  5. Book 2 after Deal — stacked kit, misattributed. Socially Vale; ontologically not a vampire. Nobody knows the source. She takes credit. Truth is Book 3.
  6. Already woke (hers, misnamed): battle-body / fork-leak / unturnable pipe. People call it “failed turn,” then “the land in him.”
  7. Danu’s LOAN while sworn: Art + SHAPE. Mental manipulation + matter. He hates how easy sex makes the Art. He uses it because he believes the war.
  8. Danu’s tap / HOST: land-flood, warp-joy. Costume of a god. Not true-god.

Butterfly lady (texture, not a cameo). Danu, not Ruth. Things that might be a street and might be inside the head. Mind control is a main theme. A lot of “magic” is a mind changing so completely the world might as well have. SHAPE exists so this is not only a dream-book: walls can fall.

Split the board: her pretty set-pieces may be Castles / Art (not-real). Innocent rooms, Drew’s body, Priya’s body, the cascade, grey vampires when the root dies = MEAT. Not a hallucination you wake from. If Book 2 becomes “none of it happened,” the grief hinge dies.

Consent: her Art is the dishonest leash. A yes that exists only because she wrote it in a head is not a yes. Mark the loaned Art.
6. Book 3 — he dumps the costume. He draws the background fabric — her kind of current, the clay under both lungs — through his own body. TRUE GOD, briefly. Not “human host of the Horned God.” A god in his own right. That is how he wins.
7. The same beat is how he dies. Deity-current through even a warped body is too much. He chooses the morning knowing that.

Do not give him Book 2–3 magic at the wake after the failed turn. Do not merge conversion with Deal or with the study. Do not wink that the blood will fail.
