5. Magic

From Planning Bible · Draft 07. Canonical whole: PLANNING-BIBLE.md. Word source: material/source/Seven_Year_King_Planning_Bible.docx.

Locked decisions are marked LOCK. Open choices are marked OPEN.

Five verbs. Everything a gifted person does is one of these, or a combination.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Verb**    **What it is**
  ----------- ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **DRAW**    Pull Aether through the self. Earth-side natives have a cap. Otherworld-touched have no cap except what the current will let through. The protagonist has a leak, not a tank. This is the Endangered / Eragon / Canavan merge.

  **SHAPE**   Impose will on matter and energy. Conservation applies. Lift a car, pay for lifting a car. Create a flame from nothing, pay the full thermal cost. High-end: pressure, heat, material manipulation, pocket-space.

  **BIND**    Share, steal, or gift life-force. Vampire feeding. Canavan higher magic. House Isador taking. A willing gift is different, morally and practically, from a theft. Repeatable siphon is how individuals become nuclear.

  **ENTER**   Project into Aether-space: dreams, Castles, the between. Mind-work lives here, not in SHAPE. You do not puppet a body like a rock. You meet a mind and rewrite from inside. Cost: you risk your own Castle. Fear makes alignment harder; intimacy makes it easier.

  **WARP**    The body becomes an overloaded conduit and distorts. Sláine's ríastrad. Early spasms are partial, ugly, terrifying. Late spasms grow antler, change bone, make him something other than human. Final form is the Horned God --- and that form is what dies. The Goddess can refuse the tap. After the deal, the Morrígan can open it.
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Laws

  • Conservation. You pay with life, borrowed life, or landscape-life. No free lunch.
  • Direction. Most beings can only work one current. Vampires BIND along the Shadow-current. Witches DRAW and SHAPE along the Earth-current. Shifters are split.
  • The protagonist exception. He can DRAW both ways. That is why both sides want him and why he can kill both.
  • Sex is a BIND + ENTER event. Orgasm drops the gate between instinct and will. The Morrígan's champion-night is the oldest ritual form of this.
  • Accepting the seven-year deal is speaking a True Name as sacrifice. The geis is a BIND on the self.

How it feels in the book

Earth-current work smells like wet soil, copper, sex, crushed green. Shadow-current work smells like cold iron, old rooms, the inside of a mouth after a nosebleed. Aether itself --- the between, his aspect --- has no smell. It is the pressure change before weather. People who stand near him when he DRAWs both ways get a nosebleed or an erection or both. That is a feature.

Gifts

From Twilight's usable part: a human trait can amplify into a unique mental gift when a person is opened to Aether (turned, sworn, or warped). One character gets a shield that answers the Art. One vampire gets emotion-push or sensory wipe. Do not give everyone a party trick. Three gifts in the whole book is plenty.

The Art, specifically

Ported from Tristan's Tale and parked under ENTER. Early on it is done to him. Mid-book he learns it and hates how easy sex makes it. Late-book he can do it without sex and chooses not to, except once. Castles can be nested. A stronger practitioner can suppress someone else's externalised self --- experienced as castration-of-self, not a stun-spell. The Shadow King works this way at cultural scale: plant a single idea until it becomes policy. Vampire courts and human institutions are his Castles.
