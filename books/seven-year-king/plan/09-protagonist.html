9. Protagonist

From Planning Bible · Draft 07. Canonical whole: PLANNING-BIBLE.md. Word source: material/source/Seven_Year_King_Planning_Bible.docx.

Locked decisions are marked LOCK. Open choices are marked OPEN.

OPEN. Name withheld on purpose at the start, per the brief. We follow experiences. A name arrives when someone claims him --- vampire house-name, witch-name, or the name he gives the Morrígan. His True Name is not any of those.

The arc, in one line

Team vamp → team Earth Goddess → both are bad, strive for balance → kills both → last night with the Morrígan → dies as Horned God → the book leaves him and stays with the crow.

The Hannaford beat lives in stages 1--2: he can wreck these people and does not, until the moment he does.

What he is, magically

Not a nature witch. Not a shadow vampire. The conduit. His aspect is the between. He can DRAW Earth-current and Shadow-current. Sex cracks the concealment on that nature --- Endangered's emergence beat, rewritten.

What he is not

  • A chosen one who was always going to be good.
  • A dark-lord-in-waiting who was always going to be bad.
  • A blank for the reader to inhabit with no interiority. Unnamed is not the same as uncharactered. Give him a specific voice, a specific hunger, a specific way of being bad at talking, a specific thing he finds funny.
