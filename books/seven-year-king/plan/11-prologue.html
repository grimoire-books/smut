11. Prologue

From Planning Bible · Draft 07, amended: ancient peek. Canonical whole: PLANNING-BIBLE.md.

Locked decisions are marked LOCK. Open choices are marked OPEN.

LOCK. Ch-00 is iron-age. The Ryan / Sion Mills / A5 / Sperrins-feeder opening is the wrong peek. Scrap it as chapter 0. It may be reused much later as a recent modern spending the papers called an incident. Not now.

Past tense. Last day only. Not the protagonist. Not a sacred king. A spent champion. Tone-set: sex and battle as twins. This is the rhyme-template for every chosen, including ours.

Where / when

Iron Age Britain or Ireland. Same islands as the modern book. A real kind of place — a ford, a ridge, a cattle-raid or shield-wall — not a generic secondary world. Not Manchester. Not the A5.

Last day

He knows the morning. He does not run. No phones, no papers, no dual carriageway. Iron, cattle, weather, a ford.

Night

She is a woman. Three sentences uncollapsed: I will use you / you will die / I want you. Treaty sex. Time-grammar only: tonight, morning, watch, bear, go. Never stay / mine / harvest. Maiden-face is a trap he refuses if three faces are used. Load-bearing.

Dawn

Iron battle. Swords, spears, axes, shields. He accomplishes one strategically essential thing. Glorious, costly, proto-warp if the body will take it. He goes down. Crow on the body.

Cut

Present tense. An unnamed man begins to wake in a damp rented house in Manchester. In the dream he was both the warrior and the crow. Do not explain the dream.

Do not explain the dream. Do not have him journal it. Do not have a helpful friend interpret it. Let it rot in him like a seed.

Forbidden on the page

  • Modern furniture, roads, phones, papers.
  • Naming Horned God, Danu, Aether, valve, outer cosmos.
  • Softening so a reread is already tender.
  • Without a throne to sit on or any Book 3 thesis in crow-POV.
  • Making him a sacred king on a seven-year lease. Only the protagonist takes the geis.
  • Origin speech. I am not of these worlds. Portal language.

Prose of this peek is not this pass. Card first. Target when allowed: 3–6k.
