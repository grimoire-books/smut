3. Book 1 ending card — the Deal (wedding before prose)

Status: provisional where marked. Lock before drafting ch. 34.

Who offers
The Earth Goddess as a woman at the threshold. Not Ruth. Ruth may have told him where to stand and talked too much and gone. The offering mouth is land-grammar: husband, years, more, the earth will have him at the end. She looks like rescue. She is not named as a hoard.

Where (provisional)
A real threshold they can reach from Palatine in one night — gritstone / a hill-edge / a stone that is not the Palatine churchyard (that one already did peek-work). Then the last image is the road toward the land (north; the old map; not a named Newgrange/Tara/Oweynagat). He walks out of the house toward that. Cairn does not bless the stone.

Who is there

  • Unnamed man (clear yes).
  • The Goddess-as-woman (offer + Deal-night).
  • Priya and Joss close enough to hear the date and the year-six plan — they wanted a wedding that meant they keep him inside a term. Drew may be there or may have said stay at the door and not come; KEEP-seed: he hates this leaving even if he smiles. Do not kill him. Do not convert him.
  • Cairn: not at the vow, or on the road and silent in a different way (losing a battery; he will not say that).
  • Morrígan: one crow. No speech. No contradiction. No comfort. Does not sell the years.

Exact sentences of the seven years (draft for lock — not prose-chapter yet)

She, cold enough to be believed:

Power now. You are mine for seven years. Consort. Weapon. Then you go into the earth for me. That is the whole of it.

He, naming the date (provisional festival; lock the calendar later):

Seven years from this night, on Samhain. I will come due then. Not before.

She does not say bursting-vessel. She does not say harvest-as-ripening-body. She may say ripe and more in the sex; the vow itself stays job-shaped.

He does not say unless. He does not look at the crow when he says the date.

Year-six plan spoken
The deposit from ch. 33, out loud: a winter in the place Priya already put money on; the person in that kitchen; the trip. Joss included. Drew told. They plan a life inside his term. He plans it with them. No wink.

Who is silent
The crow. Cairn, if present at a distance. The unnamed man does not tell the bird anything. She does not tell him he will miss the date. Book 1 has no right to that knowledge in the mouth of anyone living.

Sex
Deal-night: DRAW as flood. Different furniture from the study. Yes to a term that is a lie about when, not a no-overridden. Mouth and hour match on seven years. They do not match on the true harvest — but he does not know that, and the chapter does not.

Last image
Walking toward the land. House behind. Clock started. Still unnamed to the reader if the vow's True Name is not a name we keep. Team Hunger is the house he left. Team Earth is the road. Book 2 may begin. This volume does not.
