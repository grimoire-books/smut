## How to use the number

Do not write toward 125k. Write the locked rooms. Length is the consequence of not leaving early.

A load-bearing sex scene that changes who holds the leash is **2–5k** and should be. A civilian room after a battle is **1–3k** and should be. The grief-centre in Book 2 is **chapters, not a weekend**. That is where the extra 30k in Book 2 goes. It is not padding.

If aiming traditional UK/US adult fantasy, keep Book 1 nearer **120–130k** and let Book 2 be the one that stretches. If aiming self-pub / Kindle spicy-fantasy, **140–170k** a volume is already normal on that shelf and Book 2 can sit at the top of the band without looking strange.

**Rule of thumb for draft cuts:** if a chapter is not doing sex, war-cost, belonging, or the crow at the edge of the frame, it is a candidate. Everything else we locked has already earned its pages.
