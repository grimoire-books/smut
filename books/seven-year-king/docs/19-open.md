**Urgent**

- **Starting ground.** London, Belfast, Dublin, rural west of Ireland, split between two, or somewhere less obvious (Manchester, Glasgow, Derry, a garrison town, a ferry port)?

- **Age and class.** Twenty, mid-twenties, early thirties? Working-class, scholarship-into-elite, already inside the elite and failing at it?

- **One love interest or several.** House Isador harem (capped at two or three, not twelve) versus a single Akkarin figure versus one vampire and one witch.

- **The bully.** Nest-mate, human crucible, or witch?

- **How visible is the supernatural.** Full masquerade, leaking masquerade, or open-secret among people who know not to say?

- **Single novel or a trilogy dressed as one epic.** Lived time is now 3--4 years, which helps a single volume. A trilogy still maps onto team-vamp / team-goddess / apostasy, but risks losing the "one life, one geis" feeling.

- **Exact end-date named at the pledge.** Which Samhain or solstice does he speak aloud? Which year-six promise (place, person, winter) do the lovers believe?

- **Heat register.** Literotica-open (anatomical, long scenes, little fade-to-black) or dark-romance-literary (explicit but cut tighter)? This decision changes chapter length and who the book is for.
