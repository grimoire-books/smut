# 16. Themes

From **Planning Bible · Draft 07**. Canonical whole: [PLANNING-BIBLE.md](PLANNING-BIBLE.md). Word source: `material/source/Seven_Year_King_Planning_Bible.docx`.

Locked decisions are marked **LOCK**. Open choices are marked **OPEN**.

- Simplification is the crime. Complexity is the good. Consent is how a complex system stays alive at the scale of a body.

- Boost both realms. Extinguish neither. Kill the dictators. Leave the worlds. No third throne.

- Whether love can tell the truth about cost. This is the personal-scale message. Everything else serves it.

- Power exchange is the how. Consent is whether the how was agreed. Love is whether anyone lied about the cost. Unpick the three. Do not let a bed treat them as one word.

- Arousal is not consent. Coming is not agreement. Hate and want can occupy the same hour.

- She never pretends the leash is love. The leash is the job. The love is real and separate.

- The only moral use of power is the one that can lose what it loves. Everything else in the book is a hoard.

- Informed consent to being used --- the erotic and the tragic version of the same sentence.

- Found family that is also a structure of extraction. Feeling sincere. Metaphysics false.

- Two hoards calling themselves homes.

- Glory as a form of spending.

- A stolen future with a calendar face --- year six as a place and a person, not an abstract.

- Three heresies, one office: steal time, pay on schedule, spend the remainder against the lender.

- The body as a site the gods do not ask politely to occupy.

- Balance as labour, not harmony. The crow does not rest.

- Class. Who is battery, who is king, who only looks like either.

- The strength to bear it --- hers, and her champions'. The slow burn of the whole epic is arriving at that as love.

- Grief as rehearsal. The lovers' willing death is the first time the book shows the champion virtue in people who are not the Morrígan. He watches from the outside. Last night he has to have it from the inside.

- Guilt that does not wipe clean because they said yes. Post-traumatic growth: capacity, not hardness. He is not colder. He is able.

- Three rooms of death: innocents who did not choose; lovers who fight him; lovers who stand with him and die. He needs all three.

- The greater good is sincere in the hour and revealed as manipulation later. Reader complicity. No mid-war irony.

- Full emotional range at the extremes. Joy and tenderness have to exist or the horrors have nowhere to fall from.

- Warp-joy: he likes the power in the hour. Then he has to live with having liked it.

The book is allowed to be sincerely filthy and sincerely interested in these at the same time. That combination is the point of synthesising Sláine with the smut sources rather than writing a sexless myth or a mythless fuck.
