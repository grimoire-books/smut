**Thin places that do mythic work**

- Newgrange / Brú na Bóinne --- womb, solstice, tourist car park by day.

- Tara --- kingship, almost too obvious.

- Emain Macha / Navan Fort --- Ulster Cycle ground.

- Oweynagat, Rathcroghan --- her cave.

- Giant's Causeway / Antrim coast --- a thinning that looks like geology.

- Glastonbury / Somerset levels --- British Horned God overlay.

- Avebury, Callanish, Tintern --- secondary wells.

- London Underground / Crossrail --- Shadow-current architecture under old money.

- A multi-storey car park that should not have a standing stone in the basement.

- A pub on a crossroads that predates the Romans.

**Mundane crucible**

The Hannaford / Canavan bullying needs an adult box. Candidates: an elite graduate scheme, a City trainee house, a Dublin corporate set, a military mess, a vampire-kept artists' commune, a university department that is not for students. Pick one and let it be specific. Class has to bite. This book is partly about who gets to take and who gets taken.

**Masquerade**

The mundane world never quite sees it. A warp-spasm in an alley looks like a psychotic break. A Morrígan-battle looks like a knife-crime spike. A sídhe door looks like a locked estate. Do not hold a press conference. Do not have the Prime Minister briefed unless that is a Shadow-King joke.

**Geography of the war**

Vampires pool where money and old stone sit together: London, Edinburgh, Dublin Georgian, maybe Belfast as a contested thinning. Earth-current wells sit on the old ritual map. The motorway between them is not neutral. Ferries, bog roads, tube tunnels, and dusk at standing stones are crossings.
