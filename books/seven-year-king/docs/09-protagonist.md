**The arc, in one line**

Regular person → nest forge (will before blood) → **failed turning, unturnable** → vampire-matching champion-body, still meat → Deal (HOST in Book 2) → true-god-then-rupture (Book 3) → the book leaves him and stays with the crow.

**Kill this sentence:** *dies as Horned God.* He dies as a god in his own right, briefly, in a human pipe. He was never a stable Horned God. Book 2 HOST is Danu’s costume. Book 3 dumps it.

**What he is, magically**

Not a nature witch. **Not a vampire** --- unturnable (axiom). The conduit. His aspect is the between / clay. Book 1: leak, then forge, then failed turn, then basic champion-body (gun-rule leak only). Book 2: HOST. Book 3: true-god-then-rupture. Sex cracks concealment. Do not give him host or true-god at the wake.

**What he is not**

- A chosen one who was always going to be good.

- A dark-lord-in-waiting who was always going to be bad.

- A blank for the reader to inhabit with no interiority. Unnamed is not the same as uncharactered. Give him a specific voice, a specific hunger, a specific way of being bad at talking, a specific thing he finds funny.
